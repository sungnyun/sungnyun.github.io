Bio

I’m a postdoctoral fellow at KAIST, where I work with Prof. Se-Young Yun and Prof. Tae-Hyun Oh. I study how AI agents use vision, sound, and language to understand and interact with the world.

In the long run, I want to build intelligence that understands humans well enough to help us explore possible futures. My goal is to model how people react to one another’s decisions and predict how those interactions might shape societies and, over time, the course of human civilization.

Before studying AI, I majored in chemical engineering and industrial engineering at KAIST. I went on to earn my M.S. in 2021 and my Ph.D. in 2026 at KAIST’s Graduate School of AI, advised by Prof. Se-Young Yun. During my Ph.D., I interned with two research teams. At NAVER Webtoon AI, I worked with Junsoo Lee on multimodal text-to-image diffusion models. I later joined AWS AI Labs in Pasadena, where I worked with Haofu Liao and Srikar Appalaraju to train models that read visually complex documents using knowledge from language models and specialized document models.

My work has appeared at venues including NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, AAAI, CVPR, ICCV, ACL, EMNLP, ICASSP, and Interspeech. I was named a Top Reviewer at ICML 2025 and an Outstanding Reviewer at CVPR 2026. One of my papers on lightweight speech models received a Best Student Paper Award at ICASSP 2024.
