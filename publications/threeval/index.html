Exploring Perceptual Evaluation of Emotional 3D Talking Heads with MLLM-as-a-Judge

Hyeyeon Kim, Ji-Yeon Kim, Sungnyun Kim, Tae-Hyun Oh

Preprint 2026

  • Multimodal
  • Spatial/Social Intelligence

Abstract

A key question in evaluating emotional 3D talking heads is whether generated facial motions convey their intended emotions. Conventional geometric metrics measure fidelity to ground-truth motion but do not directly assess emotional expressivity. Automated perceptual methods, including emotion classifiers trained on 2D videos, also remain limited on rendered 3D motions. We investigate whether Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) can evaluate emotional expressivity through controlled analyses of evaluator capabilities and visual representations. Based on these findings, we establish THREEval, a 3D Talking Head Rendering-based Emotion Evaluation protocol. THREEval shows strong agreement with human judgments for facial expressions with high human consensus and ranks generation models differently from conventional geometric metrics. These findings provide guidance for MLLM-based evaluation and highlight the potential of MLLM-as-a-Judge for assessing emotional expressivity beyond geometric fidelity.
